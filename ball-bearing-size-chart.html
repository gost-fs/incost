How to read a bearing number

6 — single-row deep groove ball bearing. Second digit — the series: 0 extra light, 2 light, 3 medium. The last two digits give the bore: 00 = 10 mm, 01 = 12 mm, 02 = 15 mm, 03 = 17 mm, and from 04 on the bore is the number × 5 (6204 → 20 mm, 6310 → 50 mm).

Same bore, heavier series: larger outside diameter and width, higher load rating. 6004, 6204 and 6304 all fit a 20 mm shaft.

Suffixes

  • ZZ / 2Z — metal shields on both sides.
  • 2RS / 2RSH — rubber seals on both sides.
  • C3 — internal clearance larger than normal (hot running, interference fits).

Frequently asked questions

What size is a 6204 bearing?

20 × 47 × 14 mm: 20 mm bore, 47 mm outside diameter, 14 mm wide.

What are the dimensions of a 6205 bearing?

25 × 52 × 15 mm.

What size is a 6000 bearing?

10 × 26 × 8 mm.

What is the difference between 6204 and 6304?

Both have a 20 mm bore. 6304 is from the medium series: 52 mm outside and 15 mm wide versus 47 × 14 mm for 6204, and it carries more load.
