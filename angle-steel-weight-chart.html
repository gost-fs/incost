How the weight is calculated

The area of an equal angle is two leg rectangles minus their overlap, plus the root fillet, minus the rounded toes:

A = t·(2a − t) + (1 − π/4)·(r1² − 2·r2²) mass per metre = A [mm²] × 7850 kg/m³ × 10⁻⁶

Example, L50×50×5 (r1 = 7, r2 = 3.5): A = 5 × 95 + 0.2146 × (49 − 24.5) = 480.3 mm² → 3.77 kg/m.

Price of a profile

Multiply the mass per metre by the length and your price per kilogram, and add the cutting: InCost does this in the “Profiles and cost” section and adds the profile to the product with your prices.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 50×50×5 angle weigh?

3.77 kg per metre (2.53 lb/ft).

What is the weight of angle 40×40×4?

2.42 kg per metre.

What do r1 and r2 mean?

r1 is the root radius between the legs, r2 the radius at the toes of the legs. They change the area slightly and are part of the EN 10056-1 designation.

Do unequal angles follow the same formula?

Almost: replace 2a with a + b, the two leg lengths. This page covers equal angles only.
