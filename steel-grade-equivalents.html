Equivalent does not mean identical

Steels from different standards are made to different chemistry ranges and tested differently. Grades marked without ≈ have close chemistry and properties and are routinely substituted; grades marked ≈ only serve the same purpose. For a structural, pressure or safety-relevant part, check the mill certificate (EN 10204 3.1) against the drawing requirement, not the name.

Common pairs

  • S235JR ↔ ASTM A36 ↔ Ст3сп — general structural steel, easy to weld.
  • C45 ↔ AISI 1045 ↔ Сталь 45 — medium-carbon steel for shafts and machined parts, heat treatable.
  • X5CrNi18-10 (1.4301) ↔ AISI 304 ↔ 08Х18Н10 — the standard stainless steel.
  • X2CrNiMo17-12-2 (1.4404) ↔ AISI 316L — stainless for chlorides and food.

Frequently asked questions

What is the US equivalent of S235JR?

ASTM A36 is the usual equivalent: both are general structural steels with about 235–250 MPa yield strength.

What is C45 in AISI?

AISI 1045 (EN number 1.0503). In GOST it is Сталь 45.

Is 1.4301 the same as 304?

Yes, X5CrNi18-10 / 1.4301 is the European designation of AISI 304 stainless steel.

What is the AISI equivalent of 34CrNiMo6?

34CrNiMo6 (1.6582) corresponds to AISI 4340 and GOST 40ХН2МА.
