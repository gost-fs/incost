How metal weight is calculated

Weight is volume multiplied by density. The calculator finds the cross-section area of the shape, multiplies it by the length, then by the density of the material you pick.

Weight = Cross-section area × Length × Density Plate A = W × T Round bar A = π × D² / 4 Square bar A = S² Hex bar A = 0.866 × F² (F = across flats) Round tube A = π / 4 × (OD² − (OD − 2t)²) Rect. tube A = W × H − (W − 2t)(H − 2t) Angle A = t × (A + B − t)

Example: a 24 × 12 in plate, 1/4 in thick, in A36 steel. Volume is 24 × 12 × 0.25 = 72 in³. Steel weighs 0.2836 lb/in³, so the plate weighs about 20.4 lb (9.26 kg).

Weight of steel plate per square foot

A quick rule for carbon steel plate: each 1/8 in of thickness weighs about 5.1 lb per square foot (40.8 lb/ft² per inch of thickness). In metric, steel sheet weighs 7.85 kg per m² for every millimeter of thickness.

From weight to a price

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 4×8 sheet of steel weigh?

A 4×8 ft sheet of 16 gauge (0.0598 in) carbon steel weighs about 78 lb (35 kg). A 1/4 in thick 4×8 plate weighs about 327 lb (148 kg). Weight grows directly with thickness.

How much does steel weigh per cubic inch?

Carbon steel weighs about 0.2836 lb per cubic inch (7.85 g/cm³). Stainless 304 is slightly heavier at 0.286 lb/in³, and aluminum 6061 is about a third of steel at 0.0975 lb/in³.

Is aluminum lighter than steel?

Yes. Aluminum is about 2.9 times lighter than steel for the same size part: 2.70 g/cm³ versus 7.85 g/cm³.

How do I calculate the weight of a steel pipe?

Choose “Round tube / pipe”, enter the outside diameter, wall thickness and length. The calculator subtracts the hollow inside from the outside area and multiplies by length and density.
