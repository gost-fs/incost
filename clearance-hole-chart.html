Which series to use

  • Fine (close) — precise location, few parts, holes drilled or reamed together.
  • Medium (normal) — the default for most machined and laser-cut parts. If a drawing just says “clearance for M8”, use 9 mm.
  • Coarse (loose) — large tolerances, welded frames, holes punched or cut separately in several parts.

On laser-cut parts the medium series is usually right: the kerf and the hole quality on thick plate eat into the close fit.

Counterbores

The counterbore diameter and depth in the table let the head of an ISO 4762 socket head cap screw sit just below the surface. Add a little depth for a washer.

Frequently asked questions

What size clearance hole for M8?

9 mm for a normal fit (ISO 273 medium), 8.4 mm close fit, 10 mm loose fit.

What size clearance hole for M6?

6.6 mm normal, 6.4 mm close, 7 mm loose.

What is the clearance hole for M10?

11 mm normal (medium), 10.5 mm close (fine), 12 mm loose (coarse).

What clearance hole for a 1/4 inch bolt?

Close fit: F drill (0.257 in). Free fit: H drill (0.266 in, 6.76 mm).
