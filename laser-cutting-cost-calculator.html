How laser cutting is priced

Laser shops price a flat part from three things: the material in the blank, the time the laser spends cutting and piercing, and the setup spread over the order.

Material = blank W × H × thickness × density × (1 + scrap) × $/lb Cut length = perimeter + holes × π × d + other cutouts Laser time = cut length ÷ cutting speed + pierces × pierce time Cutting = laser time × machine rate Setup = setup cost ÷ quantity Price = (material + cutting + setup) × (1 + margin)

Why cut length matters more than size

A small bracket with many holes can cost more than a larger plain plate. Every hole adds cut length and a pierce, and piercing thick plate takes several seconds. Fewer, larger cutouts cut faster than many small ones.

Typical laser cutting rates

Most US job shops charge $80–200 per hour of laser time, plus a setup or minimum order charge of $25–75. High-power fiber lasers cut thin sheet several times faster than CO₂ lasers, so the price per part drops even when the hourly rate is higher.

Frequently asked questions

How much does laser cutting cost per hour?

Shop rates for fiber laser cutting are usually $80–200 per hour of machine time in the US, and €60–150 in Europe. The price per part also includes material, setup and margin.

How is laser cutting priced per inch?

Divide the hourly rate by the cutting speed. At $120/h and 220 in/min on 16 gauge steel, cutting costs about $0.009 per inch, plus a fraction of a cent per pierce.

Why do small parts have a minimum price?

Every job needs programming, loading the sheet and unloading parts. A setup charge spread over the order makes small quantities cost more per part.

What is a pierce and why does it cost extra?

Before cutting each contour, the laser burns a starting hole through the material. On thick plate this takes several seconds, so parts with many holes take longer to cut.

Can I get the cut length from a DXF file?

Yes. InCost reads DXF files and measures cut length and pierces automatically, including tabs, lead-ins and nested parts.
