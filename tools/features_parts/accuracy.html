Accuracy and trust

A wrong price costs real money. So every number is open.

InCost never gives you a black-box total. You see where each dollar comes from, and you can check it.

Every line is visible

Material, cutting, pierces, setup, bending, overhead and margin are shown line by line with the inputs behind them. The price justification PDF always adds up exactly to the quoted price.

Your machines, your rates

No hidden averages. Cutting speeds by power and gas, machine rates, setup times and material prices are yours to set and are saved for every next quote.

Calibrated by actual times

After a job, enter how long it really took. InCost compares plan with actual and corrects future quotes for that machine.

Checked against independent math

On real shop drawings, part areas and cut lengths match an independent calculation to within 0.0005%. Overlapping lines and gaps in your DXF are flagged before you quote. Every release passes automated tests in US, EU and metric modes.

Your data stays yours

Drawings, costs and margins stay on your PC. Only what you choose to share leaves it, such as a quote link, which carries only what your customer sees.

Safe to run

No installer and no admin rights. It works offline, and in-app updates install only if they carry the developer's digital signature.
