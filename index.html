FAQ

Questions before you download.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Free has no time limit and no account. The 4-day Pro trial starts on first launch and needs no card.

How do I install InCost?

Download the ZIP, extract it to any folder and run InCost. No installer and no admin rights needed. New versions arrive inside the app automatically.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning. Is it safe?

InCost is a new app and is not code-signed yet, so Windows may ask you to confirm. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your PC. Internet is used once to activate a Pro license.

Can I use my own machine rates and material prices?

Yes. Rates, setup costs, margins and prices per pound are all editable and saved for next time.

Does it work in metric?

Yes. InCost works in inches and millimeters, pounds and kilograms, and converts between them as you type.

How do I get a Pro license?

Buy through Lemon Squeezy and you receive a license key by email. Paste it into InCost to unlock Pro.
